# Store listing (English) — written by Vi, 28/11/2026
App title: Little Sill — By the Little Window
Short description (≤80): A peaceful Vietnamese slice-of-life game: tend plants, brew tea, and slow down.

Little Sill — By the Little Window is a gentle, zero-stress cozy game set in a quiet Saigon alleyway. There are no timers, no fail states, and nothing to rush.

Having just moved into a small attic room on Bougainvillea Lane on a rainy day, all you need to do is open the little window, water your potted Vietnamese plants, brew a warm cup of tea, pet Biscuit the cat, and listen to the quiet stories drifting in from your neighbours.

Gentle moments waiting by your window:
🌿 Tend Your Windowsill Garden: Grow 12 familiar Vietnamese plants—from daisies and moss roses to butterfly peas and touch-me-nots—press dried blooms, and brew soothing herbal teas.
💌 7 Heartwarming Neighbour Stories: Receive letters carried by a sparrow from the old clockmaker, paper planes from the tired painter upstairs, notes tied to Biscuit's ribbon from little Na, a pulley basket from Thảo's flower shop, postcards from a faraway friend, library cards from Mr. Khải's used bookstore, and evening piano melodies from Ms. Hạnh across the lane.
🌦️ Four Seasons & Vietnamese Festivals: Watch the alley change through Spring, Summer, Autumn, and Winter, and celebrate Tết (Lunar New Year), Mid-Autumn lantern parades, Vu Lan floating lanterns, and rainy-night Christmas.
🍲 Little Charcoal Stove & Crafts: Simmer traditional Vietnamese sweet soups (chè) and cakes to share with neighbours, craft pressed-flower bookmarks, and journal your thoughts at your own pace.
📮 Shareable Postcards: Capture your peaceful window view and send a postcard to a friend.

Put on your headphones, turn on the old radio, and allow yourself to slow down for a little while today.
